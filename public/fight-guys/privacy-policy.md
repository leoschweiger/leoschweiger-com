Last updated: 17 November 2026

## 2. What data is processed

**Discord user ID.** When you open the Game inside Discord, Discord tells the Game who you are. I store your Discord user ID so that your teams and results belong to you.

**Teams and play data.** Because the Game is asynchronous, I store each team you build together with a timestamp, the Game version, and your user ID. Other players can face these teams later. Monthly rankings are calculated from this data.

**Discord display name.** The Game shows your Discord display name next to your teams and in rankings. I store it together with your user ID and update it whenever you log in with a changed name.

**Local storage.** The Game saves a player identifier in your browser's local storage (Unity PlayerPrefs). It is needed for the Game to work and is not used for tracking or advertising.

**Server logs.** My hosting provider Vercel records technical request data and errors, which can include your IP address, the time of the request and the requested address. I use these logs only to keep the Game running and to find bugs.

I do not collect your avatar, email address, friends list, messages or any other Discord data. I do not show ads, I do not sell data, and I do not use your data for profiling.

## 3. Why and on what legal basis

I process your data to run the Game, store your teams and show rankings. The legal basis is performing the agreement to provide the Game to you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Server logs are processed for security and stability of the Game, which is my legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## 4. Who receives your data

- Neon (database hosting), which stores your user ID, display name and team data.
- Vercel (backend hosting), which processes requests and keeps server logs.
- Discord, which provides your identity and display name and has its own privacy policy.

These providers act on my behalf and may process data outside the EU, including in the United States. Where this happens, the transfer relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

## 5. How long data is kept

Your user ID, display name and team data are kept as long as the Game is online or until you ask me to delete them. Server logs are kept according to Vercel's retention settings, which are short.

## 6. Your rights

You can ask me at any time to give you access to your data, correct it, delete it, restrict its processing or hand it over in a portable format. You can also object to processing based on legitimate interest. To delete your data, email me your Discord user ID and I will remove your records.

You also have the right to complain to the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehoerde, dsb.gv.at) or to the authority in your country.

## 7. Age

The Game is for players aged 13 and older, in line with Discord's own minimum age. If you are under the minimum age in your country for consenting to data processing, please use the Game only with the permission of a parent or guardian.

## 8. Changes

I may update this policy when the Game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
